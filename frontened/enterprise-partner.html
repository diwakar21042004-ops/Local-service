Frequently asked questions

What are the documents and details required to start receiving bookings through ProLocal?

You will need your basic business registration, GST details (if applicable), banking information for payouts, and proof of address and identity for verification.

How long will it take for my business to go live on ProLocal after submitting the documents?

Once all mandatory documents are submitted and verified, it usually takes between 24 to 48 hours for your enterprise profile to become active and visible to customers.

What is the one-time onboarding fee? Do I have to pay it at the time of registration?

The onboarding fee varies depending on your service category. It does not need to be paid immediately; it can be deducted from your first few successful payouts.

How can I get help and support from ProLocal if I get stuck?

Our dedicated enterprise support team is available 24/7. You can contact your assigned Key Account Manager or email onboarding@prolocal.com for immediate assistance.

How much commission will I be charged by ProLocal?

We are currently offering a 0% commission period for your first month. After that, commission rates range from 5% to 15% depending on the specific service category and volume.

How will I get my payouts?

Payouts are automatically processed on a weekly basis directly to the bank account you provide during registration.
